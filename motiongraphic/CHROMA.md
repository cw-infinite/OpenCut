# CHROMA

A second, graphics-first 15-second motion film. Original geometric artwork and an original synthesized 160 BPM electronic score. Each 1.5-second stage has a distinct visual system, palette and choreography. All scenes have continuous internal movement; expanding iris transitions connect the stages. Two temporal samples per output frame provide subtle motion blur.

Deliverable: `output-chroma/CHROMA.mp4` — 1920 × 1080, 60 fps, H.264, stereo AAC, exactly 15 seconds. Editable source: `render_chroma.py`. The previous film remains in `output`.

The only on-screen words are short model/system names. Small year markers preserve the historical arc. Shapes are original abstract interpretations, not official model logos or technical architecture diagrams. The final 2026 stage illustrates agents and names two contemporary models; it does not imply agents first appeared in 2026.
